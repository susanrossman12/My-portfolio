About

I’m Susan — a copywriter and creative director who has spent my career figuring out how to make people care.

I started as a junior copywriter with a thesaurus and too much confidence, and learned the hard way that the best line in the world is worthless if it answers the wrong question. So I got obsessed with the question. Now I lead with strategy and finish with craft — the kind of writing that reads effortlessly precisely because it wasn’t.

As a creative director, I build and guide copy teams, translate a fuzzy brief into a clear idea, and protect the good stuff all the way to launch. As a writer, I still love a blank page and a deadline. My work for TD Bank alone has spanned brand, product, social, film and print — one voice across every place it needed to live.
