About

I’m Susan — a copywriter and creative director who has spent the last fifteen years figuring out how to make people care.

I started as a junior copywriter with a thesaurus and too much confidence, and learned the hard way that the best line in the world is worthless if it answers the wrong question. So I got obsessed with the question. Now I lead with strategy and finish with craft — the kind of writing that reads effortlessly precisely because it wasn’t.

As a creative director, I build and guide copy teams, translate a fuzzy brief into a clear idea, and protect the good stuff all the way to launch. As a writer, I still love a blank page and a deadline. I’ve worked across consumer and B2B, in-house and agency-side, for scrappy startups and brands you’d recognise.
