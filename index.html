About

I’m Susan—a copywriter and creative director who has spent all my career working with words, one way or another. While I still find joy in crafting my copy and working closely with designers, I find it most rewarding to guide other writers to have confidence in their own work.

I started out writing for a local legislator and then moved to a company that built writing software. From there I spent a decade at Independence Blue Cross, gaining experience in marketing communications, copywriting and team management.

My most recent adventure was joining TD Bank to become part of their first in-house creative agency. For 8 years, I helped build the team — developing the creative process, setting the editorial standards and enforcing the brand voice, all while the team quadrupled in size. And more recently, I’ve been looking at how we can best integrate Gen AI into our work and processes without losing the style and judgement that makes writing so personal.
